THE PRACTICE

Psychological care, centred around you.

Every person brings a different story, set of experiences and individual way of understanding the world.

The practice aims to provide a thoughtful and respectful environment where concerns can be explored without judgement and meaningful conversations can begin.

PHILOSOPHY

Care that begins with listening.

01

Understanding

Taking time to understand your experiences, concerns and individual circumstances.

02

Respect

Creating a professional environment where your experiences can be discussed with dignity and sensitivity.

03

Collaboration

Working together to explore concerns and identify meaningful directions for psychological support.

04

Individuality

Recognising that psychological care should respond to the person, rather than treating every experience in exactly the same way.

BEGIN WITH A CONVERSATION

Your experience matters.

If you are considering psychological support, the first step can simply be a conversation.
